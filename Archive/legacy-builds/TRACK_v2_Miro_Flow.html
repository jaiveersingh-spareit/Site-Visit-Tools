Spare-it TRACK

A Discovery & Alignment Tool for Waste System Visibility

The Opportunity

Current Challenge: When Spare-it engages with new customers, the sales and operations teams lack visibility into the customer's waste systems. This creates friction during discovery—conversations are generalized, requirements are unclear, and the implementation pathway lacks critical context.

The Solution: TRACK v2 is a lightweight, customer-friendly discovery form that sustainability teams can complete in 10-15 minutes while walking their facilities. The form captures essential waste system data—floors, stations, bins, waste streams, and photos—and creates a shared understanding between the customer and Spare-it teams.

The Handshake: This isn't just a form. It's a collaborative tool that bridges the gap between customer discovery and Spare-it's sales, operations, and success teams. The customer captures their waste setup; Spare-it gets visibility to provide more informed guidance and create a better implementation pathway for VISION.

Sales & Success Talking Points

📋 Use Case Narrative
  • Sustainability teams walk their office/facility with their phone
  • ~15 minute audit: upload floor plan, pin stations, add bins, capture photos
  • Floor plan provides spatial context for waste system layout
  • Edit floors, stations, and bins anytime—no delete/re-add required
  • Photos include bin condition + signage (contamination context)
⚡ Time-to-Value
  • ~15 min audit for typical office building
  • Mobile-first design—works on any phone
  • Data saved locally (no internet required during capture)
  • Instant export: ZIP files with photos + Excel data sheet
  • Ready for immediate internal review
🤝 Customer-Spare-it Handshake
  • Customer benefit: Simple, intuitive way to document their waste setup
  • Spare-it benefit: Visibility into customer's actual waste system
  • Alignment: Sales, Ops, and Success teams get richer context for conversations
  • Implementation: Better pathway planning for VISION rollout
  • Discovery: Questions & conversations become more productive

Cross-Team Process Flow

1
Customer Discovery Call (Sales)
Sales introduces TRACK v2 as a collaborative tool to document their waste system.
2
Customer Completes Audit (Sustainability Team)
Sustainability team walks facility on their phone, captures floors, stations, bins, photos in 10-15 min.
3
Data Shared with Spare-it (Customer → Sales)
Customer exports ZIP + Excel, shares via email or direct upload.
4
Cross-Team Review (Sales + Ops + Success)
Sales, Operations, and Success teams review data & photos. Better understanding of customer's waste system.
5
Scoped Handshake (Ops + Success)
Ops and Success teams define best implementation pathway for VISION with customer context.
6
Implementation Planning (Full Team)
Hardware placement, training, rollout timeline defined with visibility into customer's actual waste system.

Next Steps

Phase 1: Sales & Customer Success Validation
Review TRACK v2 interface and use case narrative with sales team. Identify customer personas who would benefit most. Scope out best handshake process between customer, sales, ops, and success teams.

Phase 2: Pilot with Early Customers
Deploy TRACK v2 with 2-3 customers during discovery phase. Gather feedback on UX, data usefulness, and process alignment. Refine workflow based on real-world usage.

Phase 3: VISION Integration Planning
Work with Product team to define how TRACK v2 data feeds VISION implementation planning. Explore connections between contamination context and VISION deployment.
